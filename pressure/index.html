Pressure

Does Pressure measure my blood pressure?

No. Use a cuff, then type the reading in, or let your cuff's app send it through Apple Health.

Why two readings?

Many doctors ask for two readings a minute apart. Pressure keeps the sheet open and averages them.

How do I send the report?

Tap the share button at the top of the main screen.

I bought it on another device.

Settings › Restore Purchases.

How do I get a refund?
